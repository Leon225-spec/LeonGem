Break up large pieces of information into multiple smaller ones.

This makes it easier and faster for people to understand. By reducing cognitive load, users can process your message without feeling overwhelmed by a wall of text.

Highlight key information using descriptive headings.

This allows people to quickly scan information and get a better idea of the structure and organisation of content. Good hierarchy acts as a map for the reader's eyes.

Some won't read supporting text, so make sure headings make sense out of context.

This also helps screen reader users, as they often listen to a list of all headings on a page before skipping to the information they're after. Clarity is the ultimate form of accessibility.
